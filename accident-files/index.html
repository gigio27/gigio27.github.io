Accident Files

Air and space disasters, from the official reports

Each file sums up one accident from its investigation: what happened, the timeline, what the recorders captured, the probable cause in the investigators' own words, and what changed afterwards.

  • American Airlines Flight 191, 1979A DC-10 loses an engine on takeoff at Chicago O'Hare. The damage was done eight weeks earlier, in a maintenance hangar in Tulsa.
  • PSA Flight 182, 1978A Boeing 727 hits a Cessna over San Diego. A conflict alert went off 19 seconds before, and nobody passed it on.
  • Air Florida Flight 90, 1982A Boeing 737 hits the 14th Street Bridge in a snowstorm. The gauges showed takeoff power; the engines were not producing it.
  • Space Shuttle Challenger, 1986The booster joint had been eroding for years, and the engineers recommended against launching the night before.
  • Space Shuttle Columbia, 2003Engineers asked for pictures of the damaged wing while the crew was in orbit. The investigation later judged a rescue feasible.

How these files are made

Every fact on these pages is checked against the sources listed at the end of each file, starting with the official report. Quotes from NTSB and NASA reports are US government works in the public domain. Each file ends with our documentary on the accident.
