retention-engine

A private tool used by one person for their own YouTube channel.

What it does

retention-engine produces long-form documentary videos, uploads them to its operator's own YouTube channel, and reads that channel's YouTube Analytics to learn which parts of each video hold viewers.

It signs in with Google only to act on the operator's own channel. It is not offered as a service, and no other Google account authorizes it.

Google user data
